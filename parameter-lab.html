Quick directional guide to common MRI parameter trade-offs.
← Back to Dashboard
These are common directional tendencies; exact effects depend on sequence and implementation.

FOV ↑

SNR ↑ · Resolution ↓ · Scan time ↔

Larger pixels/voxels if matrix is unchanged.

Matrix ↑

SNR ↓ · Resolution ↑ · Scan time may ↑

Smaller in-plane pixels; phase matrix can affect time.

Slice Thickness ↑

SNR ↑ · Through-plane resolution ↓

Larger voxel collects more signal.

NEX / Averages ↑

SNR ↑ · Scan time ↑

SNR improves approximately with the square root of NEX.

Receiver Bandwidth ↑

SNR ↓ · Chemical shift/distortion ↓

Wider bandwidth reduces SNR but can reduce some artifacts.

Parallel Imaging Acceleration ↑

SNR ↓ · Scan time ↓

Trades SNR for speed; performance depends on coil geometry.
